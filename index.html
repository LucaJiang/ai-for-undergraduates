Boost Your Study Skills with AI

Use AI to think better — not to stop thinking.

Part 0: Opening, risks, and first learning demo

普通话讲解。开场不要先展示炫技，而是先定调：AI 可以帮助学习，也可以削弱学习。今天的目标不是禁止 AI，而是学会有意识地使用 AI。

AI can make you perform better — and learn less.

Performance with AI ≠ learning without AI

Looks good

Finished faster
Got more answers right
Felt more confident

May be fragile

Could you solve it later?
Could you explain why?
Could you notice an error?

问学生：如果 AI 帮你把练习做对了，这是否等于你学会了？答案是不一定。这里把“成绩/表现”和“学习迁移”分开。

Getting the answer is not the same as learning how to get the answer.

这篇研究的重点不是“AI 有害”，而是没有 guardrails 的 AI 容易让学生绕过思考。学生练习时表现更好，但拿走 AI 后迁移表现变差。

But AI can also make students learn more.

Kestin et al., Scientific Reports 2025 · Harvard undergraduate physics RCT

Not an answer machine

Designed around active learning, scaffolding, targeted feedback, and self-pacing.

Better learning gains

Students learned substantially more than in the active-learning classroom condition, while using less time.

AI is not the problem. The interaction pattern is the problem.

这里马上平衡：我们不是反对 AI。好的 AI tutor 设计可以提高学习效果。今天要学的是 interaction pattern，而不是简单工具列表。

Crutch or scaffold?

Cognitive crutch

  1. Problem
  2. AI answer
  3. Copy
  4. Move on

Cognitive scaffold

  1. Problem
  2. My attempt
  3. AI hint / feedback
  4. My revision
  5. Explain it myself
用“拐杖”和“脚手架”区分。拐杖替你走路，脚手架帮助你搭建结构，最后是要拆掉的。

Live demo: AI can read your learning materials.

Screenshot → Gemini → better learning prompt

Weak prompt

Explain this slide.

Learning prompt

I am learning this topic for the first time.
Do not simply summarize the slide.

1. Identify the prerequisite idea I need first.
2. Explain only that idea with an intuitive example.
3. Ask me one question to check my understanding.
4. Do not continue until I answer.

Three risks to keep visible

THINK

Am I still doing the thinking?

VERIFY

Is the answer actually correct?

INTEGRITY

Am I allowed to use AI this way?

这三个风险从开头贯穿到结尾。不是最后才突然讲学术诚信，而是每个 demo 都回到这三个问题。

Use AI deliberately.

  1. What am I trying to achieve?
  2. Which tool fits the task?
  3. How much reasoning should I ask for?
