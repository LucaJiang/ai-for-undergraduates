STEM

For A = diag(2,1), A(1,0) = (2,0) = 2(1,0). This is an eigenvector with λ = 2. A(1,1) = (2,1); no single λ matches both coordinates, so it is not an eigenvector. For A = diag(1,0), (0,1) is a nonzero eigenvector with λ = 0. The zero vector is excluded by definition. A negative λ reverses orientation; a zero output has no direction.

Medicine

Sensitivity conditions on everyone with disease: TP/(TP+FN). PPV conditions on everyone with a positive result: TP/(TP+FP). In the original table they are 90% and 50%, respectively. At prevalence 1%, holding sensitivity/specificity 90%, use N=10,000: TP=90, FP=990, PPV=90/1080=1/12 ≈ 8.33%. At p=0 and specificity=1 there are no positives, so PPV is undefined. The lab reports expected counts and not random observations.

Business

A: revenue changes from 2,000 to 2,160, an 8% increase. Midpoint quantity change is 20/110 and price change is −2/19, giving elasticity −19/11 ≈ −1.727. Its magnitude exceeds one. B: revenue is 18×105=1,890, below 2,000; midpoint elasticity is −19/41 ≈ −0.463. Neither case establishes profit without costs, or causality without considering the design and other changes.
