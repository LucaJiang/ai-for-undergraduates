# Part 0 media notes

These clips are deliberately short. The goal is to establish the current capability frontier, not to turn Part 0 into a product showcase.

## Selected clips

### 1. GPT-Realtime-2 — realtime voice + tools

- Source: OpenAI, **Build Hour: GPT-Realtime-2**
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGS9Ghnq1RU
- Workshop excerpt: **06:58–07:28** (30 s)
- Reveal embed: `start=418&end=448`
- Why: the chapter at 06:58 begins the voice-powered search-agent demo. It is a compact example of realtime conversation connected to reasoning/tool use.
- Speaker line: **“A conversation with AI can now become an interface to tools, not just a way to produce text.”**

Fallback if YouTube fails: show the slide title and explain the capability in one sentence; do not spend workshop time debugging playback.

## Optional frontier case: Figure Helix 02

- Official source: https://www.figure.ai/news/helix-02
- Figure describes Helix 02 as a unified visuomotor system connecting vision, touch, proprioception and whole-body control.
- The official page includes an autonomous multi-minute dishwasher task and dexterous manipulation demonstrations.
- **Do not play the full demo in Part 0.** Keep it as a 15–20 second verbal example / source link in the first version of the workshop.
- Reason: the official media is embedded on Figure's site and is not as reliable as YouTube for specifying a reproducible `start/end` segment inside Reveal.js.

If a stable official or high-quality YouTube upload becomes available before the workshop, replace the static Helix slide with a ~20 second excerpt.

## Rehearsal checklist

1. Open the public deck in the same browser/network that will be used at the venue.
2. Check the three timestamp ranges and adjust by a few seconds if the strongest moment is slightly outside the current cut.
3. Confirm YouTube embeds are not blocked by campus network/privacy settings.
4. Keep each clip below ~30 seconds.
5. If a clip fails, **skip it immediately** rather than troubleshooting on stage.
6. Total capability montage target: **60–75 seconds of actual video**.

## Narrative bridge

After the montage, do not continue discussing frontier models. Move immediately to:

> **So what should you use all this intelligence for? Learning — or avoiding learning?**

This is the transition from capability to the workshop's central learning principle: AI should act as a scaffold rather than a cognitive crutch.
