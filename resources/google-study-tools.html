A final-project example

Compare spaced practice and cramming for university students.
Focus on primary studies testing recall at least one week later.
For each study, list the participants, task, follow-up and result.
Link the original papers and explain where findings differ.
Begin with a research plan for me to review.

Before starting the search, check the population, comparison and outcome in the plan. Afterward, open two important papers and compare their actual results with the report's summary.

Two useful starting points

Exam revision: begin with the material your course covers. Final project: begin with a focused question and explore relevant research. Either way, keep your own notes about what you learned.
