# Part 0 Prompt Cards

## Weak prompt

```text
Explain this slide.
```

## Learning prompt

```text
I am learning this topic for the first time.
Do not simply summarize the slide.

1. Identify the prerequisite idea I need first.
2. Explain only that idea with an intuitive example.
3. Ask me one question to check my understanding.
4. Do not continue until I answer.
```

## Critique-my-understanding prompt

```text
Here is my explanation of the concept:

[Paste your own explanation here]

Do not rewrite it immediately.

1. Identify anything incorrect or imprecise.
2. Identify the most important idea I missed.
3. Ask me one question that would reveal whether I truly understand it.
4. Only after I answer, help me revise my explanation.
```

## Evidence-sensitive prompt

```text
I want to use AI to study, but I do not want to bypass my own learning.
For this task, tell me:

1. What should I try myself before asking AI?
2. What can AI safely help me with?
3. What should I verify independently?
4. What might violate academic integrity rules?
```
