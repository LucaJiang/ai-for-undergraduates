  <p class="eyebrow">Part 5 · 16:02–16:10</p>
  <h1>Verify. Think. Stay honest.</h1>
  <p class="subtitle">The final responsibility is still yours.</p>
</section>

Note:
普通话讲解。Part 5 是结尾，不要讲成校规宣读。目标是给学生一个可执行的 checklist：什么时候要验证、什么不能上传、什么不能交给 AI 做、如何披露使用。

---

<section>
  <h2>Bring back the three risks.</h2>
  <div class="risk-row part5-risk-row">
    <div><strong>THINK</strong><p>Am I still doing the thinking?</p></div>
    <div><strong>VERIFY</strong><p>Is the answer actually correct?</p></div>
    <div><strong>INTEGRITY</strong><p>Am I allowed to use AI this way?</p></div>
  </div>
  <p class="takeaway">A good AI workflow protects all three.</p>
</section>

Note:
呼应 Part 0。学生要记住：AI 使用风险不只是作弊，还有认知外包和错误信心。

---

<section>
  <h2>Hallucination is not only fake facts.</h2>
  <div class="part5-hallucination-grid">
    <div>Wrong assumption</div>
    <div>Missing condition</div>
    <div>Fake citation</div>
    <div>Overconfident simplification</div>
    <div>Correct formula, wrong context</div>
    <div>Real source, unsupported claim</div>
  </div>
  <p class="takeaway">The most dangerous answer is often plausible, not obviously wrong.</p>
</section>

Note:
这里讲“幻觉”不要只讲编造论文。对本科生最常见的是：AI 把条件说漏、把适用范围扩大、用真实来源支持不了的结论、或者公式对但题目场景错。

---

<section>
  <h2>Use a verification ladder.</h2>
  <div class="part5-ladder">
    <div><strong>1</strong><span>Ask: what assumptions did you make?</span></div>
    <div><strong>2</strong><span>Check the exact source or lecture note.</span></div>
    <div><strong>3</strong><span>Test with a simple example.</span></div>
    <div><strong>4</strong><span>Compare with another model or textbook.</span></div>
    <div><strong>5</strong><span>Ask a human when stakes are high.</span></div>
  </div>
</section>

Note:
这页给实操。低风险问题可以只问假设；涉及考试、论文、引用、医学/法律/金融建议时，要上更高层级。注意：比较两个模型不是最终验证，只是发现问题的一种方法。

---

<section>
  <h2>Prompt: force the answer to become checkable</h2>
  <div class="prompt good full-width-prompt">
<pre><code>Before I rely on this answer:

1. List your key assumptions.
2. Mark which claims come directly from my provided material.
3. Mark which claims are your own inference.
4. Tell me what I should verify in the original source.
5. Give one simple test case or counterexample.</code></pre>

这个 prompt 很适合学生在提交前使用。重点不是让 AI 再解释一次，而是把回答拆成 assumptions / source-supported / inference / verify / test case。

---

<section>
  <h2>Protect private and sensitive data.</h2>
  <div class="part5-data-grid">
    <div class="danger"><strong>Do not upload</strong><span>IDs, addresses, patient data, classmates' work, unpublished data, confidential files</span></div>
    <div class="middle"><strong>Reduce risk</strong><span>remove identifiers, use toy data, summarize instead of uploading raw files</span></div>
    <div class="safe"><strong>Ask first</strong><span>course instructor, supervisor, university policy, data owner</span></div>
  </div>
  <p class="takeaway">If you would not post it publicly, pause before putting it into an AI tool.</p>
</section>

Note:
香港的学生可能有医学、商科、科研项目、实习材料。强调不要上传病人数据、公司资料、同学作业、未公开研究数据。这里不做法律细节，只给原则。

---

<section>
  <h2>Academic integrity: a practical boundary</h2>
  <div class="part5-boundary-grid">
    <div class="safe"><strong>Usually appropriate</strong><span>explain concepts, generate practice questions, critique your draft, improve clarity</span></div>
    <div class="middle"><strong>Check policy</strong><span>editing assignments, coding help, data analysis suggestions, citation assistance</span></div>
    <div class="danger"><strong>Do not outsource</strong><span>answers for assessed work, fabricated sources, hidden AI writing, analysis you cannot explain</span></div>
  </div>
  <p class="source">Course policy and instructor instructions override this slide.</p>
</section>

Note:
这里用 practical boundary，不讲空泛道德。特别强调：coding help 和 editing 有时允许、有时不允许；最重要的是课程要求。你不能提交自己解释不了的东西。

---

<section>
  <h2>Disclose AI use when required.</h2>
  <div class="part5-disclosure">
    <p>Keep a simple record:</p>
    <div><span>Tool</span><strong>Gemini / DeepSeek / Notebook</strong></div>
    <div><span>Purpose</span><strong>Explanation / feedback / editing / code help</strong></div>
    <div><span>Your role</span><strong>What you changed, checked, or rejected</strong></div>
    <div><span>Verification</span><strong>Sources, tests, or instructor guidance</strong></div>
  </div>
</section>

Note:
如果课程要求披露，学生应该记录工具、用途、自己做了什么修改、如何验证。不要说“AI 只是帮了一点点”这种模糊话。

---

<section>
  <h2>Final checklist</h2>
  <div class="part5-checklist">
    <div><strong>Before asking AI</strong><span>Have I tried first? What help do I want? Am I allowed to use AI here?</span></div>
    <div><strong>After the answer</strong><span>Do I understand it? Can I explain it without AI? What must I verify?</span></div>
    <div><strong>Before submitting</strong><span>Is the work mine? Did I follow policy? Can I defend every step?</span></div>
  </div>
</section>

Note:
这页建议让学生拍照。把开头的警告、Part 2/3 的学习方法和 Part 5 的诚信边界合起来。
