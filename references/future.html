Competition and research mathematics

IMO, July 2025

Google DeepMind's report on the advanced Gemini Deep Think system: five problems solved, 35/42 points, and a gold-medal-standard result certified by IMO graders.

First Proof Second Batch, June 2026

The benchmark authors' report evaluates four AI systems on ten research-level problems. Across all four systems combined, seven problems received at least one passing evaluation. Thirty expert mathematicians participated in evaluation; each submitted solution was assessed by at least two referees. Passing included essentially flawless solutions and those requiring minor revisions.

The report also describes a novel approach to one stochastic partial differential equation problem. These results motivate the discussion about learning alongside more capable AI. The slide asking about AI becoming better than every mathematician is a future hypothetical.
