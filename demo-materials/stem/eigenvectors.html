A v = λ v

Transformation

A matrix transforms a vector. Most vectors change both length and direction.

Some special vectors keep the same direction after the transformation.

Common confusion

An eigenvector is not a vector that does not move. It can stretch, shrink, or flip.

The key property is direction, not length.

Question

Why does this vector keep its direction after applying the matrix?

Learning goal

Connect the algebraic equation A v = λ v with geometric transformation.
