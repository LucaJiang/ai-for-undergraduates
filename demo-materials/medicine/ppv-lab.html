Who is in the denominator?

Sensitivity = P(positive | disease). PPV = P(disease | positive). Fixing sensitivity and specificity while changing prevalence is a thought experiment, not a claim that test performance is identical across real populations.

Independent checks

At 10% prevalence and 90% sensitivity and specificity: 900 true positives and 900 false positives, so PPV = 50%. At 1% prevalence: 90 true positives and 990 false positives, so PPV ≈ 8.3%. At zero prevalence and perfect specificity no one tests positive, so PPV is undefined, not zero.

Counts are calculated before rounding for display. Real populations can differ in factors that affect sensitivity and specificity; this page deliberately holds them fixed to isolate the role of prevalence.
