Business demo: Price elasticity

Screenshot this page and ask an AI model to help you learn from it.

Scenario A

Price decreases from $100 to $90. Quantity sold increases from 100 to 130.

Revenue changes from $10,000 to $11,700.

Scenario B

Price decreases from $100 to $90. Quantity sold increases from 100 to 105.

Revenue changes from $10,000 to $9,450.

Common confusion

Lower price does not always mean higher revenue.

Question

Why does the same price cut increase revenue in Scenario A but decrease revenue in Scenario B?
