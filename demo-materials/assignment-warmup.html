Check after your own attempt

The increase is (120 − 100) / 100 × 100% = 20%. The denominator is the original price.

If the price falls from HK$120 back to HK$100, the percentage decrease is (120 − 100) / 120 × 100% ≈ 16.7%. Now HK$120 is the starting value.
