#### **Part 1. Tool Division & "Reasoning Difficulty" Selection Guide (10 mins)**
* **Primary Tool Positioning (Accessible directly in Hong Kong):**
  * **Google Gemini:** Excellent multi-modal understanding (image recognition of notes, long PDF reading, slide organization, language polishing).
  * **DeepSeek:** Top-tier logical reasoning, code generation, in-depth explanation of concepts and mental health guidance.
* **Core Skill: AI's "Reasoning Difficulty" and Model Switching**
  * **Fast Models (e.g., Gemini Flash / DeepSeek-V3):**
    * *Scenarios:* Translation, outlining, quick summarization, daily emails, simple flashcards.
    * *Features:* Extremely fast response, straightforward; ideal for high-frequency, low-complexity tasks.
  * **Deep Reasoning/Thinking Models (e.g., DeepSeek-R1 / Gemini Thinking Mode):**
    * *Scenarios:* Complex mathematical derivations, algorithm analysis, getting stuck on proofs, multi-step logical chain breakdowns.
    * *Features:* Unfolds a "Thinking Process," self-verifies and corrects, rigorous output, takes longer.
* **Selection Principle:** Use the fast track for simple documents; always turn on "deep thinking" for hardcore math/science or long logic tasks.

#### **Part 2. Universal Study Workflow & Prompt Golden Rules (20 mins)**
* **Precision Prompt Formula:** `Role` + `Context & Task` + `Constraints` + `Format`.

  * **Speed Reading Slides & Glossaries:** Extract core arguments and generate bilingual glossaries.
  * **Active Recall:** Turn AI into an examiner to generate questions while hiding the answers.
